Creechurch House

City of London, EC3

Refurbishment of a vacant City of London building to deliver contemporary CAT A office accommodation.

MetaForm led the refurbishment of the building, transforming the vacant property into modern CAT A office accommodation. The project included the comprehensive upgrade of the office floors and building services, refurbishment of the reception and common areas, improved cycle and shower facilities, a new lift and the creation of a rooftop terrace, while retaining the character of the existing building.

Approx. 17,000 sq ft · CAT A Offices · Refurbishment
