Chelsea Reach

Chelsea, London

Repositioning of an underutilised Victorian industrial building into a contemporary office-led development in Chelsea. The consented scheme combines retained heritage fabric with substantial new-build accommodation, creating approximately 50,000 sq ft of flexible workspace with a central atrium, landscaped courtyard, roof terraces and enhanced occupier amenities.
