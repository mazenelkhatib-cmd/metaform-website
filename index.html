About MetaForm

Creating value
through real estate.

MetaForm is a London-based real estate investment and development company focused on opportunities where design, planning and active development management can unlock significant value.

We combine investment thinking with architectural and development expertise to identify opportunities others may overlook and transform them into compelling real estate investments.

Our involvement spans the full development lifecycle — from sourcing, acquisition and investment structuring through planning, design, construction and exit.

We invest alongside and work with private investors, family offices, landowners and strategic partners to deliver carefully considered projects with a clear focus on value creation and risk-adjusted returns.
